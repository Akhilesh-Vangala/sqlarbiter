# Project brief

Operator: engineer resolving a wrong business metric despite successful transformation execution. Intended outcome: diagnosis with attributable evidence, affected-output report, candidate repair and reviewed validation.

Advanced scope: original incident environment, learned diagnostic policy and deployed operational product. Business value and algorithmic advantages remain hypotheses.

Implemented scenarios: completed order-item revenue with versioned product enrichment; authoritative event-ID revenue deduplication; UTC daily counts with missing or explicit offsets. Healthy SQL and conflicting event payloads exercise no-change and clarification behavior. These controlled definitions exclude accounting revenue recognition and are not validated customer incidents.
